L27: # Opening your FHSAs
L28: 
L29: Find out who is eligible to open a first home savings account (FHSA) and how to open an account.
L30: 
L31: ## On this page
L32:

L36: ## Who can open an FHSA
L37: 
L38: To open an FHSA, you must be a 【8†qualifying individual】 by meeting all of the conditions below when you open your account.
L39: ### You meet both of the following age conditions
L40: 
L41:   * [Input] You are 18 years of age or older if the legal age in your province is 19 years old
L42: 
L43: In certain provinces and territories, the legal age at which an individual can enter into a contract (which includes opening an FHSA) is 19 years old.
L44: 
L45:   * [Input] You are 71 years or younger as of December 31 of the year you open your FHSA
L46: ### You meet the residency condition
L47: 
L48:   * [Input] You are a resident of Canada
L49: 
L50: 【9†Determining your residency status】
L51: 
L52: 【10†If you become a non-resident after opening an FHSA】
L53: ### You meet both of the following first–time home buyer conditions
L54: 
L55: You are a first-time home buyer for the purpose of opening an FHSA if, when you open the account:
L56: 
L57:   * [Input] You did not live in a qualifying home (or what would be a qualifying home if located in Canada) as your principal place of residence that you owned or jointly owned in this calendar year or in the previous 4 calendar years. What counts as a qualifying home
L58: A qualifying home is a housing unit located in Canada. This includes existing homes and those being constructed.
L59: ### Qualifies
L60: 
L61:     * Single-family homes
L62:     * Semi-detached homes
L63:     * Townhouses
L64:     * Mobile homes
L65:     * Condominium units
L66:     * Apartments in duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes, or apartment buildings
L67:     * A share in a co-operative housing corporation that entitles you to own and gives you an equity interest in a housing unit
L68: ### Does not qualify
L69: 
L70:     * A share that only provides you with a right to tenancy in the housing unit
L71:   * [Input] One of the following is true:
L72:     * You did not live in a qualifying home (or what would be a qualifying home if located in Canada) as your principal place of residence that your spouse or common-law partner owned or jointly owned in this calendar year or in the previous 4 calendar years
L73:     * You do not have a spouse or common-law partner at the time you open the account
L74: ### You need to meet all of the above conditions to open an FHSA
L75: 
L76: If you do not meet all of the conditions above, you are not a qualifying individual and cannot open an FHSA.
L77: 
L78: Based on your selections above:
L79: 
L80: ### You need to meet all of the above conditions to open an FHSA
L81: 
L82: If you do not meet all of the above conditions, you are not a qualifying individual and cannot open an FHSA.
L83: 
L84: Based on your selections above:
L85: ### You qualify to open an FHSA
L86: 
L87: If you meet all of the conditions above, you are a qualifying individual and can open an FHSA.
L88: 
L89: You will not be permitted to open an FHSA after the end of your maximum participation period. For more information on your 【11†maximum participation period】, go to 【12†When to close an FHSA】.
L90: 
L91: Example: Opening an FHSA the year you turn 18
L92: Bea is a Canadian resident who lives in a province where she is permitted to open an FHSA at the age of 18. Bea turns 18 on August 15, 2025. She will not be able to open an FHSA until that date. Once Bea turns 18, she can open an FHSA if she meets all of the eligibility conditions. Once Bea opens an FHSA, she will be able to contribute to her FHSA or make transfers from her 【13†registered retirement savings plans (RRSPs)】 to her FHSA up to $8,000 in 2025.
L93: ### Examples: Are you a first-time home buyer
L94: 
L95:   * Example 1
L96: 
L97: Aysha is a Canadian resident who is 29 years old. Aysha would like to open an FHSA on May 9, 2025. She became a joint owner of a home in 2021, which she used as her principal place of residence and sold in 2024. Aysha is not considered to be a first-time home buyer because she co–owned a home that was her principal place of residence during the period of time between January 1, 2021 and December 31, 2025.
L98: As a result, Aysha is not a qualifying individual and will not be permitted to open an FHSA until at least 2029.
L99: 
L100:   * Example 2
L101: Carlos is a Canadian resident who is 34 years old. Carlos would like to open an FHSA in June 2025. He currently lives with his common-law partner in a home that his common-law partner owns. Carlos is not considered to be a first-time home buyer because his current principal place of residence is owned by his common-law partner. As a result, Carlos is not a qualifying individual and will not be permitted to open an FHSA.
L102: ## How to open an FHSA
L103: 
L104: You can open one or more than one FHSA through an FHSA issuer, such as a bank, credit union, or a trust or insurance company. Your issuer will advise you on the types of FHSAs and the 【14†qualified investments】 they offer. For more information on the types of investments permitted in your FHSAs, go to 【15†Investments in your FHSAs】.
L105: 
L106: To open an FHSA, you must do the following:
L107: 
L108:   1. ### Contact your issuer
L109:   2. ### Provide the issuer with the information they need to register your FHSA, including:
L110: 
L111:      * your social insurance number
L112:      * your date of birth
L113:      * any supporting documents your issuer may need to certify that you are a qualifying individual
L114: 
L115: When you open your FHSAs, you may want to designate a beneficiary on your account. For more information, go to 【16†Types of beneficiaries】.
L116: If you provide information to the issuer and it is subsequently determined that you provided incorrect information, it is possible that the registration of your FHSA may be revoked as far back as the date on which it was opened.
L117: 
L118: The following will apply after the date of revocation:
L119:   * any contributions made to the account will not be tax-deductible
L120:   * any amounts transferred from your RRSPs to the account will be treated as an RRSP withdrawal and must be reported as income on your income tax and benefit return for the year of transfer
L121:   * any income earned under the account will not be tax-free and must be reported on your income tax and benefit return for the year it was earned
L122: ### File your income tax and benefit return
L123: You must fill out 【17†Schedule 15 - FHSA Contributions, Transfers and Activities】 when you file your income tax and benefit return for the year that you opened your first FHSA to let the CRA know that you opened an account, even if you did not contribute to your FHSAs or transfer property from your RRSPs to your FHSAs in that year.
L124: For more information on how to complete your Schedule 15, go to 【18†Reporting FHSA activities on your income tax and benefit return.】
L125: Example: Filing your income tax and benefit return after opening your first FHSA
L126: 
L127: Amalia is a Canadian resident who is 40 years old. Amalia has never owned a home. She is a qualifying individual and is permitted to open an FHSA.
L128: 
L129: Amalia goes to her financial institution and opens her first FHSA in June 2025.
L130: 
L131: Amalia does not make any contributions to her FHSA or transfers from her RRSP to her FHSA in 2025.
L132: When Amalia files her income tax and benefit return for 2025, she completes 【17†Schedule 15 - FHSA Contributions, Transfers and Activities】 to let the CRA know that she opened her first FHSA in 2025, even though she did not have any activity in her FHSA in that year.
L133: ## Types of FHSAs
L134: 
L135: There are three types of FHSAs that can be offered:
L136:   * a depositary FHSA
L137:     * an account (with a financial institution) that holds money, term deposits, or guaranteed investment certificates (GICs)
L138:   * a trusteed FHSA
L139:     * a trust (with a trust company as trustee) that holds qualified investments such as money, term deposits, GICs, government and corporate bonds, mutual funds, and securities listed on a designated stock exchange
L140:   * an insured FHSA
L141:     * an annuity contract (with a licensed annuity provider)
L142: For information about a type of FHSA, contact an FHSA issuer.
L143: ### Self-directed FHSA
L144: 
L145: You can set up a self-directed FHSA if you prefer to build and manage your investment portfolio by buying and selling different types of qualified investments. For more information, contact an FHSA issuer.
